Editorial Policy

The integrity of LeelinePackage rests on our independence from the suppliers and factories we cover. Brand owners, retailers and e-commerce teams rely on us for guidance that is not for sale.

Last updated: September 2026

Zero Finder’s FeesNo placement commissions accepted

  • Dogmatic Independence from Manufacturers
  • Sponsored Content and Advertising
  • Report a Violation

1Mandatory Financial Disclosures

Equity and Ownership

Any financial stake in a board mill, paper merchant, film or rigid-box supplier, molding or printing plant, logistics firm or material supplier must be disclosed.

Consultancy Ties

Current or past paid consulting for packaging brands, sourcing agents or factories — within the last 24 months — must be disclosed.

Sourcing Commissions

We do not accept finder’s fees. Where content is sponsored, it is labeled “Supplier Spotlight: Sponsored”.

2Samples and Prototype Policy

Prototype Disposal Protocol

Samples undergo destructive testing — drop, compression and rub — after which surviving units are broken down for material recycling or kept in our reference library. None are resold.

Strict No-Resale Rule

Staff are prohibited from reselling samples on any secondary market. A violation results in immediate termination and public disclosure.

Supplier Visit Ethics

LeelinePackage pays 100% of travel and lodging for every supplier audit. We never accept supplier-sponsored trips or hospitality packages.

3Dogmatic Independence from Manufacturers

What We Publish

  • Transparent lead time data, even when a factory runs behind
  • Honest pricing breakdowns, including raw material cost fluctuations
  • Supplier condition assessments based on actual audit reports
  • Labor practice evaluations drawn from BSCI and SEDEX data
  • Unfiltered pros and cons for every supplier we cover

What We Resist

  • Pressure to remove or soften a critical supplier assessment
  • Requests to inflate capacity numbers to attract more RFQs
  • Demands to hide how a labor shortage is affecting timelines
  • Threats of advertising withdrawal over a factual review
  • Offers of “exclusive access” in exchange for favorable coverage

4Sponsored Content and Advertising

Clear Labeling

All sponsored content carries a prominent “Supplier Spotlight: Sponsored” badge with a distinct visual treatment, kept fully separate from editorial recommendations.

No Ranking Influence

Sponsored suppliers cannot buy placement in Top 10 lists or editorial rankings. Paid placements live in a dedicated Spotlight section.

Full Transparency

Every sponsored article discloses the commercial relationship — compensation, samples received, or access provided — within the first 100 words.

5Report a Violation

Report an error, an undisclosed interest or a sponsored-content breach directly to us. Every report is confidential and reviewed within 48 hours.
