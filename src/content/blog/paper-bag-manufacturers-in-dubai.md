## FAQs

**1. Why are paper bags popular in Dubai?**

Paper bags have gained popularity due to the UAE's push towards sustainability and restrictions on single-use plastics. They are eco-friendly and recyclable. They are also biodegradable. Plus, they work well for branding in retail, hospitality, and food industries.

**2. Are paper bags from Dubai manufacturers eco-friendly?**

Yes, most manufacturers use FSC-certified, recycled, or virgin Kraft paper that is biodegradable, compostable, and recyclable. They often employ water-based inks and sustainable processes to minimize environmental impact.

**3. Can I get custom-printed paper bags in Dubai?**

Absolutely. Many companies provide full customization. You can choose logo printing, branding designs, and various sizes. They also offer colors and handle types like twisted, flat, or ribbon. Finishes like lamination, foiling, or embossing add a luxury touch.

**4. What is the minimum order quantity for paper bags in Dubai?**

Minimum order quantities (MOQs) differ by manufacturer. They usually range from 500 to 5,000 pieces for custom orders. This depends on factors like size, printing complexity, and type. Wholesale plain bags often have lower or no MOQs.
