01A Dimensioned SectionEvery material hatched, board thickness called out, three dimensions on the cavity, a key. The lid line is a chain line, not an edge. This is what a sample is checked against on the bench.

Two Ways a Piece Arrives Damaged

Silver darkens from sulfur in the air, and a loose piece scratches itself on the board. Both are decided by what sits under the metal and around it — long before anyone opens the box.

  1. 01

    Clearance Below the Lid Line

    Whatever the amount of padding, the piece finishes below the line where lid meets base. A closed lid that marks a stone means the insert sat too high.

  2. 02

    The Cloth Has to Stop the Air

    A sulfur-free layer sits between the piece and everything else, and touches metal rather than adhesive. This is the layer the 18-month figure comes from.

  3. 03

    The Cavity Has to Match the Piece

    Cut to the actual band, stone and clasp — held to ±0.5 mm — and the piece cannot shift sideways on the way.
