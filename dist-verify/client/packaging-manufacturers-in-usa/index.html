10 Best Packaging Manufacturers in USA for Small Business Orders

By Lofty ShenSenior Packaging Engineer·September 1, 2025·11 min read

The U.S. packaging industry, valued at $184.65 billion in 2021, is projected to grow to $218.12 billion by 2027 with a 2.94% CAGR. It plays a vital role across food, pharmaceuticals, and e-commerce, driving innovation and eco-conscious solutions.

This complete guide will get into the 10 best packaging manufacturers in USA for small business orders in 2025. Business owners can find the ideal packaging partner to protect products and improve their brand identity.

1. WestRock

Smurfit Westrock, formed in July 2024 through the merger of Smurfit Kappa and WestRock, is one of the leading packaging manufacturers in USA. Operating in 40 countries with 500 production facilities and over 100,000 team members, it stands as a global packaging giant.

WestRock Key Packaging Solutions

Smurfit Westrock offers a wide range of paper-based packaging solutions. These solutions support brands at every stage of their sales cycle. Their product range has:

  • E-commerce Packaging: The Pak On Demand™ Automated Mailer System makes custom paper mailers that are 100% curbside recyclable. These mailers give businesses an eco-friendly option instead of plastic packaging. The system creates packages that fit perfectly and cuts waste by up to 26% compared to standard sizes.
  • Retail and Display Solutions: Their shelf-ready packaging makes products more visible and helps prevent stockouts. These designs open, display, and restock easily. The innovative Meta® series products switch smoothly between different packaging formats.

WestRock Pricing & Order Flexibility

Smurfit Westrock stands out among USA packaging companies with their on-demand systems. Businesses can make custom-sized packages whenever needed. A single pallet of KD FOLD™ material produces over 1,300 mailers in different lengths (12”-20”) and widths (12” or 16”).

Small businesses can access their digital preprint technology since order minimums start at just one roll.

Contact WestRock

2. International Paper

International Paper, one of the largest packaging manufacturers in USA, operates 200 box plants nationwide, providing both national reach and local service. Its merger with DS Smith in January 2025 has positioned it as a global leader in sustainable packaging solutions.

International Paper Key Packaging Solutions

The company provides several packaging options that fit different business needs:

  • Corrugated Packaging: These solutions are reliable, renewable, customizable, and recyclable—sourced sustainably
  • E-commerce Solutions: The company excels in supply chain expertise. Their specialized e-commerce packaging reduces risk, cuts costs, and drives sales
  • Innovative Designs: Their patented Bow Tie Shipper shows their innovation. It needs no glue joints, fixes skewing issues, and creates great unboxing experiences

International Paper Pricing & Order Flexibility

Small businesses with lower order volumes will find good options here. The Bow Tie Shipper works great when orders don’t meet minimum limits for standard, glued boxes. This design fits more units per pallet than standard containers.

Small business customers save money on shipping as a result.

The company bases prices on quantity and offers discounts for quick payment. Discount periods start from when carriers receive deliveries, products reach destinations, or correct invoices arrive.

3. Ball Corporation

Ball Corporation one of the packaging manufacturers in USA, produces over 107 billion recyclable aluminum beverage cans annually, more than any other company. In 2024, it sold its aerospace business to BAE Systems Inc. for $5.60 billion, enabling a complete focus on aluminum packaging.

Ball Corporation Key Packaging Solutions

The company’s aluminum packaging portfolio includes:

  • Aluminum Cans: Multiple formats including Standard (12 oz. to 19.2 oz.), Sleek (7.5 oz. and 12 oz.), Slim (5.5 oz. to 8.4 oz.), and King (24 oz. and 25.4 oz.)
  • Aluminum Bottles: Alumi-Tek® bottles serve on-the-go consumers who buy coffee, protein shakes, and water
  • Aluminum Cups: Ball Aluminum Cup® uses 90% recycled content, the highest in its category

The company’s printing technologies use specialty inks, temperature-sensitive text, and color-changing effects. These features, along with Day-Glo graphics, help products stand out on store shelves.

Contact Ball Corporation

4. Berry Global

Amcor’s acquisition of Berry Global in April 2025 solidified its position among leading packaging manufacturers in USA.

Berry Global, previously a Fortune 500 company, was a major global manufacturer and marketer of plastic packaging products, employing over 46,000 people across 265 facilities worldwide, with revenues exceeding $14 billion in fiscal year 2022.

Berry Global Key Packaging Solutions

Berry’s packaging lineup included these impressive solutions:

  • Customizable Packaging: The Chameleon bottle brought new options with textured finishes. It came in ten standard versions, and companies could get custom designs through quick-change tooling
  • Sustainable Options: The B Circular range used up to 100% post-consumer recycled plastic and was built for circularity
  • Specialized Films: Berry’s Printed Shrink Film team spent over 20 years making vibrant printed films. These films protected products and helped brands stand out

Berry Global Pricing & Order Flexibility

Berry Agile Solutions combined their M&H and PET Power businesses to help small businesses. They offered quick turnaround times and small minimum orders. Standard bottles and closures took 4-6 weeks, while custom solutions needed 8-12 weeks.

Small businesses could order as few as 10,000 units, which made Berry’s products available to startups and companies with limited needs.

Contact Berry Global

5. Crown Holdings

Crown Holdings, one of the prominent packaging manufacturers in USA, has roots dating back to 1892.

Headquartered in Tampa, Florida, the company combines eco-friendly practices with innovative design, operating 189 plants across 39 countries with a global workforce of approximately 25,000 employees.

Crown Holdings Key Packaging Solutions

Crown’s product line includes various metal packaging options:

  • Beverage Packaging - Aluminum beverage cans make up over 60% of Crown’s business, and this format shows record growth
  • Food and Aerosol Packaging - A strong North American tinplate business serves food, beverage, and personal care markets
  • Transit Packaging - These products protect valuable items during shipping across industries
  • Promotional Packaging - Decorative metal tins are available in round, oval, and octagonal shapes. They feature innovative finishes, like HoloCrown™ holographic foil technology.

Metal packaging’s recyclable nature stays central to Crown’s operations.

Crown Holdings Pricing & Order Flexibility

Crown secured $700 million in senior unsecured notes at 5.875% interest in early 2025. This financial strength supports their manufacturing capabilities. Data about minimum orders for small businesses remains scarce.

The company seems to prefer larger production runs, given their major clients like Anheuser-Busch InBev, Coca-Cola, and Pepsi-Cola.

Contact Crown Holdings

6.Graphic Packaging International

Graphic Packaging International, a leading packaging manufacturers in USA, is a Fortune 500 corporation headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia.

It designs and produces consumer packaging from renewable or recycled materials, operating a global network of design and manufacturing facilities that support well-known brands in food, beverages, foodservice, household goods, and other products.

Graphic Packaging Key Packaging Solutions

Graphic Packaging’s product range has:

  • Paper-Based Packaging: Their paperboard folding cartons support leading brands in food, beverage, personal care, healthcare, and beauty industries
  • Foodservice Items: A variety of packaging for on-the-go needs. This includes paper containers, cups, plates, and buckets.
  • Barrier Technology Solutions: Products that offer the right oxygen and moisture barriers. They meet strict shelf-life needs.

Graphic Packaging Pricing & Order Flexibility

Small orders use POD Pricing (Proof Of Delivery pricing). Customers can see this through written notices and price lists. This program comes with specific conditions and minimum annual purchase requirements.

The company handles full electronic POD Pricing credit submissions in 30 days or less. Qualified distributors need to provide 30 days written notice for pricing adjustments based on market conditions.

7. Packaging Corporation of America

Packaging Corporation of America started in 1959 and stands out from bigger competitors by serving smaller customers with greater flexibility. PCA is the third-largest producer of containerboard and corrugated packaging in the U.S.

It makes over 5 million tons of containerboard each year and has about 10% of the domestic market.

PCA Key Packaging Solutions

PCA creates custom corrugated packaging solutions that match every specification and budget. Their products include:

  • Conventional shipping containers and custom-printed corrugated boxes
  • Interior packaging designs that protect products during transport
  • High-impact retail packaging with various print methods to promote brand messages

PCA Pricing & Order Flexibility

PCA bought Deseret Container in Salt Lake City, a supplier known for low-volume, quick-turnaround orders. This purchase helped PCA better serve small businesses with limited order quantities. The company connects with customers of all sizes.

It understands their goals to provide the right packaging solution, on time and within budget.

PCA Best Fit for Small Businesses

Small businesses want personal attention and the benefits of nationwide scale. PCA is their ideal partner. The company’s simple approach stands out for businesses that need corrugated packaging, particularly in food and beverage industries. Local teams offer on-site support.

Small businesses gain from PCA’s deep expertise and reliable supply chain, which is backed by seven mills.

8. Sonoco

Sonoco stands as one of USA’s most diverse packaging manufacturers. The company started its journey in 1899 by producing paper yarn carriers in Hartsville, South Carolina. Today, after 125 years, Sonoco employs around 22,000 people and runs more than 300 operations worldwide.

Sonoco Key Packaging Solutions

Sonoco’s portfolio brings together a wide range of packaging solutions:

  • Consumer Packaging: The division offers rigid paper containers (paperboard canisters), metal cans, and plastic packaging for food, beverages, and household products.
  • Industrial Paper Packaging: This segment makes paperboard tubes, cores, and cones. It also produces paper-based protective packaging and uncoated recycled paperboards.
  • Sustainable Options: The EnviroSense™ line includes cans made from 100% recycled paperboard. It also offers recyclable mono-material flexible pouches.

The company leads globally in producing tubes, cores, and fiber concrete columns.

Sonoco Pricing & Order Flexibility

The company adjusts prices for paperboard converted products to match rising costs. Their FirmaLoad™ bulk bag carriers come in kit form, which helps customers save money. A single trailer can hold components for 3,000 carriers compared to just 540 wood pallets.

Small businesses can manage their costs better with this flexibility.

Sonoco Best Fit for Small Businesses

Food, beverage, household, and personal care sectors’ small businesses find great value in Sonoco’s offerings. The company’s green practices appeal to businesses looking for sustainable packaging solutions.

Companies needing specialized industrial packaging like reels, spools, and protective solutions for temperature-sensitive products will appreciate Sonoco’s expertise.

Contact Sonoco

9. Sealed Air

Sealed Air, a prominent packaging manufacturers in USA, began in 1957 when Alfred W. Fielding and Marc Chavannes invented BUBBLE WRAP®, initially intended as wallpaper but recognized for its superior protective qualities.

Headquartered in the USA, the company now operates 105 manufacturing facilities across 117 countries, generating annual net sales of $5.40 billion.

Sealed Air Key Packaging Solutions

The company’s product range includes:

  • Protective Packaging: Their main products include BUBBLE WRAP® brand, mailers, inflatable packaging systems, and suspension packaging.
  • Food Packaging: CRYOVAC® barrier materials that extend food life, boost safety, and create better shelf appeal
  • Digital Printing: Options that enable variable printing, seasonal promotions, and last-minute customization

Sealed Air Pricing & Order Flexibility

Small businesses can quickly buy popular solutions through Sealed Air’s dedicated portal. Customers needing custom solutions can reach out directly through their “let’s get in touch” option. A real-life example shows how an online retailer saved $500,000 by optimizing their packaging.

People Also Ask About Packaging Manufacturers in USA

1. What is the typical lead time for custom packaging orders in the USA?

Lead times usually range from 2 to 8 weeks. This depends on design complexity, material availability, and the manufacturer’s production capacity.

2. Do USA packaging manufacturers offer custom packaging solutions?

Yes, most manufacturers offer custom packaging services. They provide unique designs, sizes, branding, and materials. This helps meet specific client needs, often with design support available in-house.

3. What regulations must packaging manufacturers in the USA follow?

They must follow FDA rules for food and drug packaging. They also need to meet EPA guidelines for environmental impact. Plus, they should stick to industry standards like ISO 9001 for quality management.
