10 Top-Rated Packaging Manufacturers in China: Factory-Direct Guide 2025

By Lofty ShenSenior Packaging Engineer·September 5, 2025·11 min read

Why is China’s packaging industry a global powerhouse? China is known for its huge manufacturing power and advanced technology. It also focuses more on sustainability. Because of this, China is a leader in packaging solutions.

With a projected CAGR of 13.5% from 2021-2026, China’s packaging market is a cornerstone of global supply chains. Its scale and innovation make it essential for businesses seeking high-quality, sustainable packaging solutions.

Picking the right Chinese packaging manufacturer can make or break a brand’s success. This piece dives into 10 top-rated packaging manufacturers in China. You’ll learn about their specialties, certifications, and green practices to help you make smart sourcing decisions.

1. YUTO Packaging

YUTO Packaging, a 28-year-old premier provider of superior packaging solutions in China, started its journey in Shenzhen in 1996. The company went public on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 2016 (stock code: 002831) and now employs over 20,000 people at 49 production sites.

YUTO Packaging specialties

YUTO delivers integrated one-stop packaging solutions in six core sectors: Consumer Electronics, Wine & Spirits, Personal Care, Food, Healthcare, and Tobacco. Their services go beyond traditional packaging and include:

  • Brand positioning and concept design
  • Innovation solutions covering graphic, structural, shape, material, and process aspects
  • Smart manufacturing that combines automated production with advanced information technology

YUTO Packaging certifications

YUTO follows strict ISO9001 Quality Management System and BRC global standards. Their eco-friendly brand YUTOECO has earned several international certifications such as:

  • DIN CERTCO and OK Compost Home from Europe
  • USDA Certified Biobased label and BPI certification from the United States
  • ABA certification from Australia

3. JIELONG Printing and Packaging

JIELONG Printing and Packaging, a 50-year old company founded in 1973, has become one of leading packaging manufacturers in China. The company operates as a key part of Shanghai Jielong Industry Group Co., Ltd.

JIELONG Packaging specialties

JIELONG creates a wide range of packaging solutions, including:

  • Color corrugated boxes and fine color cartons
  • Card paper boxes and POP displays
  • Micro-fluting products with exclusive direct-offset technology

JIELONG Packaging certifications

Quality stands at the heart of JIELONG’s success, backed by several certifications and awards. The company holds ISO9001 Quality Management System and ISO14001 Environment Management System certifications.

China Packaging Technology Association has named JIELONG as an Appointed Packaging Unit.

JIELONG Packaging sustainability practices

JIELONG takes environmental responsibility seriously with a clear focus on developing quality, eco-friendly products. The company leads the ‘Offset-to-Flexo Green Printing Transformation’ project that balances quality with environmental care.

4. MYS Group Co., Ltd.

MYS Group Co., Ltd., based in Shenzhen, has become a revolutionary force among packaging manufacturers in China since 2000. The company, listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 2009 (stock code: 002303), established itself as a complete packaging solution provider worldwide.

MYS Packaging specialties

MYS Group excels at research, development, production, and sales of various packaging solutions. Their main focus areas include:

  • Transportation packaging products
  • High-quality packaging products
  • Labeling products
  • Die-cutting products of electronic functional materials

MYS Packaging certifications

The company has earned several key certifications to ensure quality:

  • ISO9001 Quality Management System
  • ISO14001 Environmental Management System
  • OHSAS18001 Occupational Health and Safety Management

MYS Packaging sustainability practices

MYS Group’s core values emphasize “high quality, high efficiency, environmental protection and innovation”. Their eco-friendly initiatives include:

  • FSC-CoC certification for responsible forestry practices
  • Eco-friendly packaging and printing services that minimize environmental impact
  • Complete testing through ISTA and nationally certified laboratories for packaging reliability

Contact MYS Group Co., Ltd.

5. SHANYING International

SHANYING International, a leader among packaging manufacturers in China, has been listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange since 2001 (stock code: 600567).

The company excels as an integrated enterprise, combining recycled resource recovery, paper manufacturing, packaging customization, and industrial Internet platforms.

SHANYING Packaging specialties

SHANYING International makes industrial packaging paper and specialty paper at several manufacturing bases in China. Their products include:

  • Industrial packaging paper: kraft liner, corrugated paper, coated kraft paper, and white top liner
  • Specialty paper: newsprint, bag paper, and cotton swab paper

SHANYING Packaging certifications

SHANYING’s quality standards are confirmed by several certifications:

  • ISO9001/ISO14001/ISO45001 certification
  • Forest Certification
  • CNAS certification
  • Intellectual property rights system certification
  • Energy management system certification

SHANYING Packaging sustainability practices

SHANYING puts environmental responsibility first. The company recovers more than 8 million tons of recycled fibers each year, which reduces CO2 emissions by over 12.64 million tons annually.

SHANYING International’s five papermaking bases have completed EATNS carbon management system certification. The company has created carbon-neutral products to help consumers live more eco-friendly lifestyles.

Contact SHANYING International

6. HEXING Packaging

Ningbo Hexing Packaging Group Co., Ltd., a 25-year-old leader among packaging manufacturers in China, has grown into a complete packaging solution provider. The company operates four production bases near Ningbo port for convenient transportation.

Its five factories cover over 5,000 square meters and generate an annual output worth more than $38 million USD.

HEXING Packaging specialties

Hexing stands out in making paper-based products of all types. They excel at creating color boxes, corrugated boxes, paperboards, and paper bags. Their production power gets a boost from cutting-edge equipment that includes:

  • Heidelberg 5-color offset press and German Roland 5-color offset press
  • Production lines for 2-layer through 7-layer corrugated boards
  • Fully automatic film laminating, die-cutting, and gluing machines

HEXING Packaging sustainability practices

Hexing takes environmental responsibility seriously through groundbreaking approaches to eco-friendly packaging. They put eco-friendly materials first, especially FSC-certified options from responsibly managed forests.

Their color printing boxes showcase the perfect balance of beauty and sustainability. These boxes use safe, biodegradable inks. They break down naturally and keep prints bright.

Hexing pushes for 100% recyclable printed paper mailer boxes instead of traditional plastic packaging. These boxes offer durability while staying biodegradable.

Contact HEXING Packaging

7. TETRA PAK China

TETRA PAK, a world leader among packaging manufacturers in China and globally, specializes in food packaging and processing solutions, with China as its biggest market and fastest-growing innovation center.

The company’s worldwide net sales reached 12.8 billion euros in 2023, with the Asia-Pacific region contributing 33% of total revenue.

TETRA PAK Packaging specialties

TETRA PAK China provides complete packaging solutions for dairy, beverages, plant-based products and more. Their range has different sizes, shapes, and new solutions for aseptic, chilled, and food production.

TETRA PAK Packaging certifications

TETRA PAK’s quality and sustainability commitment shows through its many prestigious certifications. The company earned full FSC Chain of Custody certification. This allows them to supply FSC-labeled packages around the world.

Their environmental management systems meet international standards for packaging artwork, printing, coating, finishing, and logistics.

TETRA PAK Packaging sustainability practices

TETRA PAK shows strong environmental responsibility in all operations. The Hohhot plant runs on 100% renewable electricity, mostly from wind power. This saves about 16,000 tons of CO2 each year.

Sales reached 8.8 billion plant-based packages and 11.9 billion plant-based caps in 2022, up 24% and 12% from 2021. Using plant-based plastic saved 131 kilotonnes of CO2 compared to fossil-based options.

Contact TETRA PAK China

8. JINGJIA Group

Shenzhen Jinjia Group Co., Ltd, a 27-year old company, made history as China’s first listed enterprise in cigarette packet printing. Today, this packaging giant operates from a smart eco-industrial park in Shenzhen. It spans 250,000 square meters.

JINGJIA Packaging specialties

JINGJIA Group excels at developing and producing premium packaging products. Their main focus lies in cigarette packets, electronic supplies packaging, and daily necessities.

JINGJIA Packaging certifications

JINGJIA’s facilities maintain strict quality management systems. The company holds several industry certifications that confirm its excellence. JINGJIA ranks first among China’s top 100 printing enterprises consistently for three evaluations.

Their achievements include more than 200 social awards like “Leading Packaging Enterprises in China” and “National High-tech Enterprise.”

JINGJIA Packaging sustainability practices

JINGJIA Group’s ESG Risk Rating assessment rates their material risk management as “Weak.” Notwithstanding that, the company employs 4,204 full-time staff members. Their overall ESG Risk Rating puts them at 52nd position out of 381 companies in the Commercial Services industry group.

Contact JINGJIA Group

9. HUCAIS Group

HUCAIS Group, a 35-year old company based in Xiamen, has grown to 18 production facilities throughout China. The company started as a printing business and has evolved into a diverse packaging enterprise. Today, it employs more than 5,000 people with yearly revenue of over RMB 1.5 billion.

HUCAIS Packaging specialties

The company’s product line includes high-end cigarette boxes, wine packaging, and luxury packaging solutions. HUCAIS has built strong relationships with big brands such as Meizu, Oppo, Yanghe Classic Blue, and Yuxi Manor.

Their advanced “Tiger Wing Smart Printing” platform creates custom small-batch packaging. This platform uses technologies such as 3D direct printing, platinum embossing, and smart cold stamping.

HUCAIS Packaging sustainability practices

The company’s BSCI certification proves its commitment to international labor standards and ethical business practices. HUCAIS values clear supply chain management. It asks suppliers to follow specific sustainability guidelines.

The company uses eco-friendly materials and energy-saving methods. This helps protect the environment and ensures quality products.

Contact HUCAIS Group

10. JINGXING Paper Co.

Zhejiang Jingxing Paper Co., Ltd. started as a small paper factory in the Hangjiahu Plain of the Yangtze River Delta in 1984. The company became a leading packaging manufacturer in China. It went public on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 2006.

JINGXING Packaging specialties

Jingxing’s expertise lies in industrial paper, household paper, paper packaging, and pulp production. The company’s portfolio features kraft linerboard, white-faced kraft paper, corrugated base paper, and recycled tissue papers.

They have 5 lines for packaging paper, 5 for household paper, and 3 for printing and die-cutting. The company launched a deinking pulp line that processes office waste and old books with a capacity of 200 bdt/d.

JINGXING Packaging sustainability practices

Jingxing’s environmentally responsible approach shows in their recycled household paper production that uses renewable raw materials and clean production processes. The company’s deinking technology makes clean, safe white paper. Their natural paper remains unbleached for healthier uses.

Contact JINGXING Paper Co.

FAQs

Q1. Who are some of the leading packaging manufacturers in China?

Some top packaging manufacturers in China include YUTO Packaging, JIELONG Printing and LeelinePackage. These companies provide various packaging solutions. They are industry leaders because of their innovation and quality.

Q2. What certifications do reputable Chinese packaging manufacturers typically hold?

Reputable packaging manufacturers in China usually have certifications. They often hold ISO 9001 for quality management, ISO 14001 for environmental management, and FSC certification for responsible forestry practices. Some have industry-specific certifications.

For example, BRC is for food safety, and NADCAP is for aerospace applications.

Q3. How are Chinese packaging manufacturers addressing sustainability concerns?

Many Chinese packaging manufacturers are adopting sustainability practices. They use recycled materials and invest in renewable energy. They also work to reduce water use and create biodegradable packaging options.

Q4. What advantages does sourcing directly from Chinese packaging manufacturers offer?

Sourcing directly from Chinese packaging manufacturers has benefits. It can save costs, allow for more customization, and provide direct access to specialized manufacturing skills.
