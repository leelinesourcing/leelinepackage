## The 6-Step Supplier Qualification Plan

To find reliable **packaging manufacturers in uk** or abroad, you need a defensible specification. Factories cannot guess your needs. Follow this checklist to turn your intent into a contract-ready RFQ.

**Step 1: Define Your Spec Like a Factory**

Do not send a photo and ask for "something like this." Define the physical DNA of the packaging.

- **Substrate:** Specify the exact material. Choose E-flute corrugated, folding carton, or flexible film.
- **Dimensions:** List internal usable space versus external shipping size to prevent fit issues.
- **Print Coverage:** Select 1-color flexo for economy or 4-color litho for premium impact.
- **Performance:** Require ISTA drop tests to ensure the box survives the courier network with your product inside.

**Step 2: Audit Certifications (Evidence Over Logos)**

Verify claims with current documentation. A logo on a website proves nothing.

- **Quality Management:** Request the supplier's valid [ISO 9001 certificate](https://www.iso.org/iso-9001-quality-management.html) to verify their process consistency.
- **Sourcing:** Demand the [FSC Chain-of-Custody](https://fsc.org/en/chain-of-custody-certification) number for paper products.
- **Food Safety:** Check the latest [BRCGS audit grade](https://www.brcgs.com/our-standards/packaging-materials/) for any food-contact items.
- **Recycled Content:** Request declaration docs to prove 30% recycled plastic content.

**Step 3: Reality Check the RFQ**

Uncover hidden costs in the commercial terms. Unit price is only half the equation.

- **MOQ Bands:** Request pricing at 500, 1,000, and 5,000 units to identify price break points.
- **Tooling Fees:** Confirm if print plates and cutting dies are one-off fees or recurring costs.
- **Lead Times:** Ask about specific capacity limits during Q4 peak seasons.

**Step 4: Calculate Total Landed Cost (TLC)**

Compare suppliers using this formula: **Unit Price + Freight + Duty + Quality Inspection + Compliance.**

- **Plastic Tax:** Factor in the [Plastic Packaging Tax](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-if-you-need-to-register-for-plastic-packaging-tax) of £217.85 per tonne for packaging with less than 30% recycled content.
- **EPR Fees:** Add Extended Producer Responsibility costs based on total packaging weight.

**Step 5: When to Source in the UK**

Choose local partners for speed and risk control. Stick to domestic suppliers if:

- **Speed is Critical:** You need restocking in days rather than weeks.
- **Hands-on QA:** You want to visit the press to approve colors personally.
- **Agile Replenishment:** You cannot afford to tie up cash in six months of inventory.

**Step 6: When to Source Globally**

Select overseas partners for scale and complex finishes. Consider global sourcing if:

- **Volume is High:** Unit cost savings offset the shipping fees.
- **Complex Finishes:** Manual assembly for rigid boxes costs significantly less overseas.
- **Scale-up:** You need to move from 500 units to 50,000 units seamlessly.

## People Also Ask About Packaging Manufacturers In UK

**Are UK packaging manufacturers always faster than overseas suppliers?**

Not always. While domestic shipping takes only 1–3 days, many UK factories have production queues of 4–6 weeks due to limited capacity. "Faster" depends on the complexity of your box. Complex hand-assembled rigid boxes take longer to produce in the UK due to labor shortages.

For urgent launches, overseas production combined with air freight can sometimes beat a clogged UK production schedule.

**What certifications should I require for food packaging suppliers?**

Mandatory safety validation is critical. Require **BRCGS for Packaging Materials** (Grade A or AA) to ensure the factory meets strict retail hygiene standards. Do not accept general ISO 9001 alone; it does not cover food safety protocols.

You must also request migration test reports to prove that inks and glues will not leach chemicals into your product.

**What is a realistic MOQ for custom cardboard boxes in the UK?**

Expect Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs) to start around 1,000 units for professional flexo or litho printing. Digital printing allows lower runs (250+ units) but often doubles the unit cost.

Always ask for quotes at two tier levels (e.g., 500 and 2,000) to identify the "price break." If you need fewer than 500 units, buying stock boxes and applying custom labels is usually the only viable commercial option.

**How do I compare UK vs China sourcing without missing hidden costs?**

Calculate the Total Landed Cost (TLC), not just the factory price. You must add sea freight, UK import duty (usually 0–6%), and the [**Plastic Packaging Tax**](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-if-you-need-to-register-for-plastic-packaging-tax) for plastic components. Do not forget hidden "risk costs" like third-party quality inspections and warehouse storage fees.

UK sourcing saves on logistics; China sourcing significantly lowers the cost of labor-intensive finishes like foil stamping and manual assembly.

**What should I request in a first email to a packaging supplier?**

Be specific to avoid delays. Include the box style (e.g., FEFCO 0427 mailer), **internal dimensions** (Length x Width x Depth), and target quantity. Specify the material grade (e.g., E-flute corrugated) and attach a low-res artwork file if you have one.

Always include your delivery postcode and "in-hands" deadline. This allows the estimator to calculate freight and check production capacity immediately.
