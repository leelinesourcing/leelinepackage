Who We Are

One Plant in China, Shipping to Four Continents

A Chinese manufacturer that brands visit, audit and reorder from — rather than a trading desk standing in the middle.

LeelinePackage has been making packaging in China since 2015. Orders leave for North America, Europe, Asia and Australia, from a first run of 500 units to brands reordering by the container.

The 20,000 m² plant turns out more than a million units a month. Hand-finishing sits beside automated lines, so detail lands where it shows and volume lands where it counts — and the box shipped in December matches the proof signed in January.

What We Make

One-Stop OEM and ODM, From Brief to Loading Dock

Four capabilities cover most briefs. All four run in-house, so nothing gets quoted by one company and produced by another.

Custom Packaging Design

Folding cartons, rigid gift boxes, mailers, labels and stickers, drawn around the product first. Send artwork or send a sketch — the structure is settled before anything is quoted.

Advanced Printing & Finishing

Heidelberg presses for high-resolution print, then foil stamping, embossing, UV and lamination. Colour is matched once and held, so a reorder still sits beside the original.

Industry-Specific Boxes

E-commerce, cosmetics, jewellery, wine and candles, each with the liners and inserts the product asks for: moisture-resistant liners, velvet inserts, builds that survive the channel.

Sustainable Packaging

FSC-certified board, soy-based inks and recyclable paper, run under ISO 14001. In 2024 the plant worked at 22% recyclable material, with a 50% target set for 2026.

The Founder

Built in 2015 to Take the Guesswork Out of Sourcing

Ray spent more than a decade in global trade before he started LeelinePackage. Buying packaging back then meant juggling a designer, a printer, a materials broker and a freight forwarder — four companies, four sets of promises, and the buyer holding the risk when any of them slipped.

In 2015 he built the opposite: a single plant in China that draws, proofs, prints, finishes and packs, with one team on the hook for the result. Brands stopped coordinating suppliers and started placing orders.

The rule has not moved in ten years. Recyclable materials where the brief allows, quality that repeats run to run, and a named person on the other end of the email who already knows the order.

RayFounder, LeelinePackage

Our Certifications & Qualifications

Firstly we love packaging! We kind of have to, to be doing what we are doing. We are proud to be supplying great quality packaging. Internationally recognized standards that validate our commitment to quality, sustainability and excellence.

  • ISO 9001:2015

    Our quality management system meets global standards. This ensures we have consistent, high-quality production and keeps our customers happy.

  • BRC Packaging Certified

    Global Standard for Packaging Materials certification ensures our products are safe for food contact and meet hygiene requirements.

  • FSC Certified

    Forest Stewardship Council certification ensures our paper and wood products come from responsibly managed forests that provide environmental benefits.

  • SGS Verified

    Independent inspection, verification and testing confirms our products meet quality and performance standards across global markets.

  • ISO 14001:2015

    Shows our commitment to environmental management. We aim to minimize our impact on nature with sustainable practices.

  • ISTA-3A Compliant

    The International Safe Transit Association tests show our packaging keeps contents safe during shipping. This cuts down on damage and waste.
