The brief

When This Is the Right Order, and When It Is Not

Ten lines decide it faster than any brochure. The left column is the brief this plant is built for; the right belongs with a different supplier.

A Good Fit If

  • The run starts at 500 units, and the bag is meant to be carried more than once.
  • There is artwork to print, or a sketch to work from.
  • The fabric has to be documented — GOTS on the cotton, GRS on the rPET.
  • Four to six weeks fits ahead of the launch date.
  • The bags ship with the boxes, mailers or pouches from the same plant.

Probably Not a Fit If

  • The order is under 500 units, or only a sample is wanted with no run behind it.
  • The bags are needed inside a fortnight.
  • A stock bag is enough, with nothing cut to your own size.
  • The print has to be applied to a finished bag.
  • The tote itself has to be food-contact certified.

Questions

Before You Send an Enquiry

Six things a buying team asks once the fabric and the size are settled.

01Is the bag printed before it is sewn, or after?

Before, and panel by panel. The body is printed as flat panels, then assembled — a panel takes ink right to its own edge. A bag printed after assembly cannot reach the corners, and the ink sits differently on a curved surface.

02Can one design cover canvas, jute and nonwoven?

The artwork carries over; the ink does not. A screen that sits sharp on canvas spreads on a nonwoven — the same file is separated differently for each fabric, and a fresh proof is approved on each one.

03How are the totes packed for freight?

Flat and counted, nested into cartons on a pallet pattern drawn around the container. Bags are wrapped against moisture before the carton is strapped, because a fabric tote crosses more weather than a box does.

04What are the payment terms?

A deposit confirms the order and the balance clears before the goods leave the plant. T/T and L/C are both accepted, and the split is written into the price rather than agreed at the end.

05Does a reorder start at the same minimum?

No. The 500-unit minimum applies to a first run in a new fabric and artwork. A reorder on the same build starts lower, because only material and machine time are being bought.

06Can I see a sample before the full run?

Yes. Your size can be sewn as a white dummy to check dimensions, and a printed sample in the final fabric follows for colour. The sample charge is credited against the production order.
