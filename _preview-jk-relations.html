Linings and Anti-Tarnish Protection

Silver dulls in a container weeks before a shop ever sees it, and it is the cloth in contact with the metal that decides the outcome. Four cloths, a single job each.

  • VelvetRings and branded retail boxes · a polished stone meets nothing abrasive

  • EVA or foamTransit and mixed-SKU trays · freight cannot move the piece
  • Satin over cardNecklaces and certificates · a bright surface, a deliberate chain

What the Outside Is Made Of

The outside is where the brand is read and where most of the cost sits. Four decisions, and each one is settled against the other three.

  1. 01

    Greyboard

    1.5–3.0 mm

    Rigidity comes from the board, and each layer above it is measured from its thickness.

  2. 02

    Kraft and recycled stock

    FSC chain of custody

    A recycled sheet that still takes foil cleanly, for a range sold on what it leaves behind.

  3. 03

    Leather and leatherette

    Grain to order

    A grain that hides handling marks — that is what survives a counter better than a flat wrap.

  4. 04

    Foil, deboss or print

    Matched to Pantone

    Settled with the wrap, because each method bites differently into a texture and foil is the one that flakes.
