People Also Ask About Folding Carton Manufacturers In Texas​

1. What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for a custom folding carton run?​​

MOQs differ by manufacturer. Some specialize in small-batch or prototype runs (e.g., 1,000 - 5,000 units), which is ideal for startups or product launches.

Larger manufacturers may have higher MOQs (e.g., 25,000+ units) to be cost-effective. Be sure to ask about MOQs when requesting quotes.

2. Can Texas manufacturers handle food-grade and compliant packaging?​​

Absolutely. Many Texas manufacturers have extensive experience producing food-grade packaging. They can help with FDA-compliant materials and inks.

Some facilities might also have certifications like GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices) or comply with SQF (Safe Quality Food) standards.

3. What factors influence the cost of folding cartons from Texas manufacturers?

Costs depend on material type (e.g., standard vs. premium board), quantity, custom features (like windows or coatings), and print complexity.
