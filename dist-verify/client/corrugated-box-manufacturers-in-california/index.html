People Also Ask About Corrugated Box Manufacturers in California

1. What types of corrugated boxes are commonly produced in California?

Many corrugated box manufacturers in California produce regular slotted containers (RSC), die-cut boxes, full-overlap (FOL), half-slotted containers (HSC), telescopic boxes, displays (PDQ, counter displays), and heavy-duty triple-wall boxes for industrial and agricultural use.

2. Do corrugated Box manufacturers in Califormia have minimum order quantities (MOQs)?

Yes. Large national companies often require 5,000–25,000+ boxes per run. Smaller and mid-sized California plants, especially those with Bobst die-cutters, often accept MOQs as low as 500 to 2,000 pieces. This makes California appealing for small and medium businesses.

3. Are there eco-friendly or sustainable corrugated box options in California?

Yes. Most manufacturers offer 100% recycled content board (typically 30–100% post-consumer waste), SFI or FSC-certified options, and many have switched to water-based inks and starch-based adhesives.
