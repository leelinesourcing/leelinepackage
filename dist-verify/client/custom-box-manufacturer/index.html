Protection engineering

The Cheapest Place to Stop a Return Is the Box

A crushed carton costs the product, the freight and the customer. Structure is settled before the press runs, because the flute, the board and the fit decide what survives a sorting belt.

Engineering rule

Find the failure in the lab, not after the first complaint.

Four ways a carton usually loses

Crushed corners

Double-wall BC flute on the faces that carry the stack.

A corner takes the first hit whenever a pallet is set down, so the flute is doubled exactly where the load lands.

Rattle inside

A die-cut cradle that holds the item still, however it lands.

The insert is cut to the product rather than padded around it, and nothing shifts on a rough leg of the route.

Moisture in transit

A coated liner and a tight, sealed fit for the damp routes.

Damp air softens corrugated from the inside out; the liner seals moisture off before the board gives.

Freight paid on air

Board right-sized to the product, not to the pallet.

Empty space is volume paid to ship, and paid again on the next order.

The structure is signed off to ISTA 2A on the sample

Lab before launch

ISTA 2A is a partial-simulation protocol for parcels up to 68 kg: atmospheric conditioning, compression, vibration and shock.

Drop

A free-fall from the height the route actually hands a parcel.

Compression

A stack load past the pallet those boxes will sit under.

Vibration

Truck and rail shake, run long enough to loosen a marginal fit.

Humidity

Damp air, where corrugated gives up its strength first.

FAQ

Before the First Order

1Can I approve a dieline before production starts?

Yes. A cut file and a 3D mockup are shared for sign-off before any board is cut, and the run follows the dieline that was approved.

2How is the price of a custom box worked out?

Board grade, box size, print method, finish and run length set the unit price. Send the quantity and the artwork, and the quote itemises each line.

3Can the inside of a box be printed?

Yes. Inside print, a coloured liner and a printed insert are all available, and they show the moment the lid comes off.

4Can the boxes ship flat to save freight?

Folding cartons and many rigid styles are delivered flat or knocked down, and a container carries far more than it would with assembled boxes.

5What if a batch does not match the approved sample?

Where the variance sits with us, the run is remade or the order credited. The approved sample is the reference for the whole batch.
