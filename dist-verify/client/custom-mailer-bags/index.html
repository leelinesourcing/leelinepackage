Film

Four Films, and the Print That Goes on Them

A mailer is a film with a print on it. Those two choices decide what the pack weighs, what it protects, and how the artwork reads at arm’s length.

PE Film (LDPE / HDPE)

Low-density film gives a soft, flexible bag; high-density film a crisper, stiffer build. Both resist water and take print straight onto the surface.

Best forThe default for apparel and accessories

Bubble Lining

A layer of air cells bonded between two film faces. It is the difference between carrying a fragile item and protecting it.

Best forElectronics, cosmetics, jewelry

Kraft Paper

A paper face bonded over the protective core, for a bag that photographs as premium and recycles in the paper stream.

Best forPremium retail and gifting

Recycled and Compostable

Film made from recovered material, or a film that composts after use, giving a sustainability claim that can be printed on the bag itself.

Best forBrands with published eco targets

Printing on any film

Three routes onto the same film, chosen by the artwork and the length of the run.

  • Flexographic

    The economical route for flat two-color work at volume.

  • Screen

    Dense, opaque coverage where a flat color has to dominate.

  • Digital Inkjet

    Full-color artwork with no plate cost, for shorter runs and changing designs.

Pantone matching runs across all three routes, and the color code is best supplied before the proof goes out — that is what keeps a screen rendering and a printed film in step.

Questions

Five Questions Before the First Quote

Everything else a buyer usually asks is already answered further up this page.

01Can I send a reference bag instead of a full spec?

A photograph, a stock bag you already like, or a dimensioned sketch is enough to open the quote — nothing has to be drawn to scale on your side.

02Can a single mailer size cover more than one product?

Often, yes, and it is worth asking before the quote is written. A single gusseted size frequently covers a whole range, which takes rolls off a packing bench and simplifies reordering.

03How is the film thickness chosen?

Thickness is set alongside the size rather than assumed. A heavier film costs more per bag and takes more of a knock before it tears, and the two are quoted together.

04Do the bags arrive flat, ready for our own bench?

Mailers are supplied flat, on the roll or boxed. They store in a fraction of the space a built pack needs, and they are sealed at the bench where the order is picked.

05What artwork do you need from us?

A logo or artwork file starts the job. Panel layout, print method and Pantone codes are worked out with you, and none of it is left to guesswork.
