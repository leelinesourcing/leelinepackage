  1. What industries do corrugated carton box manufacturers in Mexico primarily serve?

They support agriculture (like fresh produce and perishables), food and drinks, electronics, automotive, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, consumer goods, and e-commerce. This helps boost Mexico’s export-driven economy and nearshoring trends under USMCA.

  1. Are corrugated carton boxes from Mexico sustainable?

Yes, many corrugated carton box manufacturers in Mexico emphasize sustainability. Companies like Titán, Smurfit Kappa, and PCM use recycled paper, FSC-certified materials, and processes that reduce waste.
