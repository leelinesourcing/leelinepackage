Amber Packaging Industries LLC started in 2006 and has become a leading flexible packaging manufacturer in the UAE. The company’s 17-year trip has led to a strong position in providing innovative packaging technology and solutions for clients worldwide.

The company now operates facilities in Toronto, Riyadh, and Ahmedabad, expanding beyond its UAE roots.

Amber Key Offerings

Amber Packaging stands out as a one-stop solution provider that specializes in tailor-made flexible packaging materials. Their product portfolio includes:

  • Flexible packaging for various sectors including meat, fish, dates, dairy, coffee, tea, and pharmaceuticals
  • High-barrier vacuum shrink bags and pouches for extended shelf life
  • Multilayered film options tailored to specific product requirements
  • Advanced rotogravure printed laminates with up to 11 colors
  • Retort grade pouches for maintaining nutrient integrity

Amber Customization Options

Plastic technologist Mr. Dharmdev Bhatt’s leadership has helped Amber excel in creating customized packaging solutions. The company’s in-house capabilities convert printed roll-fed film into various preformed pouches. Customers can select from various pouch styles.

These include stand-up, quad-style, lay-flat, fold-over, side-gusseted, spouted, and box pouches. Quad pouches offer more space for branding, while spouted pouches make it easier to use food and drinks.

Amber Pricing

The company is ISO 9001:2008, HACCP, BRC, and SEDEX certified. It upholds quality standards while offering competitive prices.

ENPI Key Offerings

United Security handles advanced security printing for banking, retail, transport, and aviation sectors.
