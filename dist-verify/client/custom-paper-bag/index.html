Questions Buyers Ask Before Ordering

01What file should the artwork arrive in?

A vector file with the dieline on its own layer, or a print-ready PDF at 300 dpi with 3 mm of bleed. Keeping the cut file separate from the design means a size change never means redrawing the artwork.

02How are the payment terms set?

A deposit confirms the order and the balance clears before the goods leave the plant. T/T and L/C are both accepted, and the split is written into the price rather than agreed at the end.

03Who books the freight, and what terms does the price use?

Carriage is quoted against the destination port on FOB, CIF or DDP, whichever the buying team works in, and the term is named on the quote. Air is priced separately when a launch cannot wait on a sailing.

04Will you sign an NDA before we share the design?

Yes. A confidentiality agreement is signed before a design file is opened, and a brand&rsquo;s artwork is never reused as a sample for another client.

05Does a reorder start at the same minimum as a first run?

No. The minimum applies to a first run of a new design; a reorder that reuses the same die and plates starts lower, because the tooling is already made and only board and press time are being bought.
