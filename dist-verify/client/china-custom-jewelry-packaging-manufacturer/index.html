People Also Ask About China Custom Jewelry Packaging Manufacturer

1. What types of custom jewelry packaging can I order?

Most China custom jewelry packaging manufacturer can produce ring, earring, necklace, bracelet, and set boxes, as well as pouches, bags, and display boxes. Many also offer coordinated collections so your boxes, pouches, and retail bags share the same branding.​

2. What materials are commonly used?

Typical materials include rigid grey/white chipboard, coated or textured paper, kraft paper, velvet, suede, PU leatherette, microfiber, and specialty fabrics. Inserts are usually EVA, foam, molded pulp, or wrapped cardboard to secure jewelry pieces.​

3. Can I customize the design with my logo and colors?

Yes, factories often offer full customization. This includes logo printing, embossing, debossing, foil stamping, UV printing, and custom Pantone color matching. You can request custom sizes, structures (like drawer, clamshell, or magnetic), and interior layouts to fit your collection.

4. What is the typical MOQ (minimum order quantity)?

MOQs often range from about 300–1,000 pieces per size or design, depending on structure and materials. Some China custom jewelry packaging manufacturer offer lower MOQs on standard or in‑stock styles (for example 100 pcs) to help smaller brands test the market.​
