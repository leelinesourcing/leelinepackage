## 1. Expanded Polystyrene (EPS) Foam for Packaging

<figure class="post-figure"><a href="https://www.leelinepackage.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="https://img.leelinepackage.com/blog/media/2025/10/Expanded-Polystyrene.webp" alt="Expanded Polystyrene" width="900" height="600" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a></figure>

Among the various types of foam for packaging, EPS stands out as a top choice. Here's why: it's 98% air, making it very light. This saves you money on shipping and handling.

**Key Benefits for B2B Packaging**

- **Excellent Insulation**: EPS keeps thermal conductivity at 0.035–0.037 W/(m·K). Your products stay protected in temperatures from -17°C to 43°C. Based on my experience, this range covers most shipping conditions.
- **Dimensional Stability & Water Resistance**: EPS changes less than 2% under different conditions. It absorbs very little water. This protects your goods from warping or moisture damage.
- **Customizable Cushioning**: You can adjust performance by changing part shape or processing settings. This gives you flexible packaging solutions. You won't need expensive retooling.
- **Compliance & Safety**: EPS meets ASTM C578 standards. You can treat it with antistatic or flame-retardant agents. I recommend this for electronics and sensitive industries.

## 2. Polyethylene Foam (PE Foam)

<figure class="post-figure"><a href="https://www.leelinepackage.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="https://img.leelinepackage.com/blog/media/2025/10/Polyethylene-Foam.webp" alt="Polyethylene Foam" width="900" height="600" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a></figure>

PE foam is better than many other types of foam for packaging. You can pick from densities of 1.2–9.0 lb/ft³. Most packaging works well with 1.2–2.2 lb/ft³. It resists water (less than 0.05 lbs/ft² absorption). It handles temperatures from -70°C to 80°C.

This covers most B2B shipping needs.

**Performance and Benefits for Your Packaging**

- **Superior Shock Absorption**: I suggest PE foam for cushioning electronics, auto parts, glass, and precision tools. It cuts damage risk during shipping.
- **Custom Fit and Application**: You can get it in sheets, rolls, custom die-cuts, adhesive, and molded forms. This protects your products well and saves space.
- **Thermal & Chemical Resistance**: It has good insulation (0.29 Btu/hr/inch/ft²/°F). This makes it perfect for shipping food or delicate items. It resists most solvents and oils. Antimicrobial options exist too.

Based on my experience, PE foam cuts product loss. It boosts customer satisfaction. It gives you a clear green advantage in your packaging strategy.

## 3. Polyurethane (PU) Foam

<figure class="post-figure"><a href="https://www.leelinepackage.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="https://img.leelinepackage.com/blog/media/2025/10/Polyurethane-PU-Foam.webp" alt="Polyurethane (PU) Foam" width="900" height="600" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a></figure>

Polyurethane (PU) foam is one of the versatile types of foam for packaging. It protects sensitive items. Its open-cell structure fits the shape of your products.

**Key Benefits for B2B Packaging**

- **Density Options for Precise Protection**: Choose from many densities to match your product's weight and fragility. High-density PU foam resists repeated drops. Low-density versions cushion delicate goods gently.
- **Firmness Customization (IFD)**: You can select foam by Indentation Force Deflection (IFD) ratings. Soft foam protects fragile parts. Firm foam supports heavier items.
- **Excellent Shock and Impact Absorption**: PU foam absorbs and spreads shocks. This reduces returns from damage. It protects your reputation.
- **Superior Moisture & Chemical Resistance**: This foam guards against water, humidity, chemicals, and mildew. I suggest it for moisture-sensitive or high-value goods.
- **Custom Fabrication**: You can die-cut, glue, or saw it for custom-fit inserts. It comes in ESD and anti-static variants, multiple colors, and densities. These meet your specific needs.
- **Compliance**: PU foam meets strict MIL and ASTM specifications. This matters for defense and regulated industries.

PU foam compresses to save space. It's lightweight. Based on my experience, it lowers your shipping costs. At the same time, it boosts product protection.

## 4. Ethylene-Vinyl Acetate (EVA) Foam

<figure class="post-figure"><a href="https://www.leelinepackage.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="https://img.leelinepackage.com/blog/media/2025/10/Ethylene-Vinyl-Acetate-EVA-Foam.webp" alt="Ethylene-Vinyl Acetate (EVA) Foam" width="900" height="600" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a></figure>

EVA foam is one of the top choices among types of foam for packaging for products that need both protection and versatility. It has a lightweight, closed-cell structure. This structure offers excellent shock absorption. It provides flexibility. It resists chemicals and moisture.

You get reliable protection from temperatures between −30°C and +70°C. This makes EVA suitable for shipping sensitive electronics. It works well for medical equipment, food, and fragile items.

**Key Features and Benefits for B2B Packaging**

- **Customizable Protection**: You can shape EVA foam into inserts, trays, or blocks. It works perfect for high-value medical analyzers.
- **Durability & Cushioning**: EVA foam has low density. It features a fine-cell structure. It dampens vibration well. EVA withstands moisture, UV, impacts, and harsh chemicals.
- **Market and Sustainability**: The global market grows steadily. It reached USD 11.06 billion in 2023. East Asia holds a 32.8% share.

## 5. Expanded Polyethylene (EPE)

<figure class="post-figure"><a href="https://www.leelinepackage.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="https://img.leelinepackage.com/blog/media/2025/10/Expanded-Polyethylene-EPE.webp" alt="Expanded Polyethylene (EPE)" width="900" height="600" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a></figure>

EPE gives versatile, lightweight protection as one of the efficient types of foam for packaging. It is a closed-cell, semi-rigid foam. It weighs 40% less than many other foams. This cuts your shipping costs and package weight. You get a high strength-to-weight ratio.

This means you can use thinner walls and smaller packages. Your packages stay economical without losing product safety.

**Core Benefits for Your Packaging Operations**

- **Superior Cushioning**: EPE gives high impact and shock resistance. It handles fragile items and electronics well. It absorbs energy consistently. I suggest it for repeated shipments.
- **Surface-Safe Protection**: EPE is non-abrasive. It shields glossy, painted, or sensitive surfaces. I've seen it work great in automotive and electronics sectors.
- **Thermal and Chemical Resistance**: EPE insulates temperature-sensitive goods. It protects perishable items for cold-chain logistics. It also resists acids, alkalis, and solvents in industrial use.
- **Dimensional Stability & Durability**: EPE keeps your precision packaging in shape. It delivers long-term reliability for reusable containers.
- **Sustainable & Customizable**: The material is recyclable and CFC-free. You can tailor it for tight tolerances, inserts, and custom pads. Based on my experience, this supports green initiatives and meets product-specific demands.

## 6. Cross-Linked Polyethylene (XLPE) Foam

<figure class="post-figure"><a href="https://www.leelinepackage.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="https://img.leelinepackage.com/blog/media/2025/10/Cross-Linked-Polyethylene-XLPE-Foam.webp" alt="Cross-Linked Polyethylene (XLPE) Foam" width="900" height="600" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a></figure>

This foam, one of the durable types of foam for packaging, has a closed-cell structure. It resists water, chemicals, and mechanical stress better than standard PE foam. Based on my experience, this foam keeps its shape and thickness even after many uses.

I suggest it for reusable packaging and high-value shipments.

**Key Benefits for B2B Packaging**

- **Superior Cushioning**: XLPE foam absorbs shock well for electronics, medical devices, and automotive parts. It protects sensitive items from impacts and vibration.
- **Water & Chemical Resistance**: This foam has near-zero water absorption. It has high chemical resistance. Goods stay safe during transport, even in wet or harsh conditions.
- **Thermal Insulation**: It maintains stable temperatures for temperature-sensitive products.
- **Customizable Options**: You can get densities from 1.5 to 9 lb/ft³ (2 lb/ft³ is standard). I like that it comes in various colors and complex shapes. XLPE adapts to your specific packaging needs.
- **Flame Retardancy & ESD**: Choose flame-retardant or ESD grades for compliance and extra safety.

**Industry Applications & Performance Data**

- **Medical**: I've seen it used in compression-molded trays and instrument protection. It supports gamma sterilization. It is hypoallergenic.
- **Electronics & Automotive**: Custom inserts and case pads work well for repeated shipments.
- **Reusable Packaging**: Low compression set (&lt;10%), moisture absorption under 0.5%, thermal conductivity of ~0.035 W/m·K.

I believe this gives you a high-performance, reusable solution. It reduces waste. It protects goods. It strengthens your packaging approach where reliability and toughness matter most.

## 8. Anti-Static Foam

<figure class="post-figure"><a href="https://www.leelinepackage.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="https://img.leelinepackage.com/blog/media/2025/10/Anti-static-Foam.webp" alt="Anti-static Foam" width="900" height="600" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a></figure>

Anti-static foam protects sensitive electronics during shipping and storage. This foam dissipates electrostatic charges. It reduces the risk of product failures from static discharge.

**Key Benefits and Applications**

- **Range of Foam Types**: You can choose from anti-static PE foam (up to 6–7mm thick), EVA foam (up to 50mm), EPE foam (up to 80mm), and classic pink PU foam (up to 50mm). Each type offers custom cushioning based on your needs.
- **Efficient for Single-Trip Shipping**: These foams work well for single-use in ESD containers and boxes. They reduce product loss. They also cut down customer returns.
- **Enhanced in Humid Environments**: Anti-static additives like GMS migrate to the surface. This boosts conductivity and protection. I find this valuable in humid climates.

Selecting anti-static foam ensures your electronics reach customers intact. This supports your brand's reputation. It also reduces warranty claims.

## 9. Pick and Pull Grid Foam

<figure class="post-figure"><a href="https://www.leelinepackage.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="https://img.leelinepackage.com/blog/media/2025/10/Pick-and-Pull-Grid-Foam.webp" alt="Pick and Pull Grid Foam" width="900" height="600" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a></figure>

Pick and Pull Grid Foam gives you great flexibility for packaging items of any shape. These foam sheets come pre-scored in **1/2" or 1" grids**. You just remove cubes to fit your products. No special tools needed. No extra setup required.

Just pinch and pull the foam to create protective spaces.

**Key Benefits for Your B2B Packaging**

- **Fits Any Product:** You can customize it for electronics, tools, glassware, and sensitive equipment. The design adjusts to work for single shipments or mixed collections.
- **Protection and Reusability:** Made from **polyurethane foam** at **1.2 pcf density**, it resists crushing. You can reuse it. It protects your goods from shock. It won't scratch surfaces.
- **Kit and Box Pairings:** You can combine it with a **200# B Flute box** for extra strength. Or add eggcrate toppers for full coverage.
- **Fast, Low-Cost Prototyping:** You avoid custom tooling costs. This works great for frequent product changes. It speeds up order turnaround.

## 10. Phenolic Foam

<figure class="post-figure"><a href="https://www.leelinepackage.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img src="https://img.leelinepackage.com/blog/media/2025/10/Phenolic-Foam.webp" alt="Phenolic Foam" width="900" height="600" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></a></figure>

I recommend phenolic foam for fire safety in packaging. It sets the standard among all foam types. You get high fire resistance with an oxygen index of 42–43. This is much higher than polyurethane or polystyrene.

The foam won't melt or drip. It gives off very little smoke (density of 9). It produces almost no toxic byproducts. This protects your goods. It also makes your warehouse safer during transport or storage.

**Key Advantages for B2B Packaging**

- **Thermal & Water Resistance:** Low thermal conductivity (0.0258 W/m·K at 35mm thick) keeps your products insulated. You get reliable protection from –180°C to +180°C. This suits extreme conditions. Water absorption is very low (0.02 kg/m³). This ensures consistent performance.
- **Mechanical Strength:** Densities range from 40–235 kg/m³. Pressure resistance reaches 216 kPa. This makes sturdy packaging for high-value or sensitive goods.
- **Custom Applications:** You can get slabs, pipe shells, boards, or profiled sheets. These come with paper, foil, or glass facings. I suggest these for fireproof panels, cold storage, and protective packaging.
- **Green and Safe:** The foam contains no CFCs or HCFCs. It resists acids and alkalis well. I find this ideal for sustainable, demanding environments.

## People Also Ask About Types Of Foam For Packaging

**1. What is Polyurethane (PU) foam and when should it be used?**

PU foam is soft, dense, and very versatile. It offers great cushioning and can be molded for a custom fit. This makes it ideal for protecting oddly shaped items and for high-impact applications.

**2. What is Cross-linked Polyethylene (XLPE) foam?**

XLPE foam is a strong type of PE foam. It’s durable, resists chemicals, and recovers well from compression. This makes it great for heavy-duty industrial packaging.

**3. How does Expanded Polyethylene (EPE) foam differ from standard PE foam?**

EPE is a low-density foam made from PE beads. It’s softer and more compressible, offering great thermal insulation.

**4. What are the advantages of using EVA foam for packaging?**

Ethylene-Vinyl Acetate (EVA) foam is soft and flexible. It absorbs shocks well and feels rubbery. This makes it great for custom inserts in cases or boxes.
