Quick route

Pick the Step You’re Stuck On

Four quick routes. Each names the spot where a project usually stalls, then takes you to the page that fixes it — or straight to a quote.

No box design yet

A product and a target cost, but no dieline yet. Send the outer dimensions, the packed weight and a photo, and we draw the structure around it — the carton style, the board grade and the insert all picked to keep the box cheap to run and still protect the product.

The box shape decides what comes after it — so front-load the structure.

A design that might not print

A logo or a low-res file that needs placing and correcting for the press. We fix bleed, trapping and colour, and catch the details that break a print run — a panel that falls on a fold, a finish that shifts the shade, or a thin rule that only holds at one size.

Press-ready artwork means no surprises on press or on the shelf.

Need physical proof first

Hold, fit and sign off a piece before approving a run. We send a white dummy to prove the fit and the assembly, then a colour prototype once the artwork is locked, so the board, finish and coating are judged on the real thing — not on a screen or a guess.

A physical sample stops a problem before it becomes a whole run.

Ready for a full quote

The design is locked and you know the volume. Send size, weight, quantity and channel — plus any deadline or retail target — and we price it on the actual structure and board, break down every line of the quote, and name the risks to watch before you commit a run.

Get a real price band and a recommended path — not a generic ‘contact us’.

One Project, End to End

A heavy glass serum bottle kept arriving broken. Here is how one carton walked end to end in about three weeks.

01StructureA heavy glass serum bottle was rattling inside a loose lid-and-base box. We redrew it as a one-piece folded carton with a fitted insert, so the bottle stays off the corners and the dead air is gone.Board kept at 350gsm — lighter than the old build, still passed a 1 m drop.
02ArtworkThe brand artwork had to move off a fragile exterior edge onto a face that wears well in a cart. Foil landed on the front panel, and colour mapped to one Pantone plus a spot UV.The proof matched the on-screen design before the plate was cut.
03SampleFirst a white dummy in 4 days to confirm the fit — no plate cost yet. Then one colour prototype over two rounds to match the proof.Signed off and cleared to the line in about three weeks.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat types of packaging do you make?

Corrugated and folding cartons, rigid gift boxes, mailers, paper and tote bags, and soft-pack pouches. The board, film and finish are matched to the protection the product needs. Share a reference pack or a photo and we’ll tell you honestly whether it’s a fit before you pay anything.

QIs a smaller-than-MOQ test run possible?

Our production MOQ is from 500 units, but we can run a lower first quantity or a small trial batch for market testing at a slightly higher unit cost. Volume breaks apply at 1k, 5k and 10k. Physical samples are available before you commit to a full run.

QHow is the price structured — are there setup, plate or tooling fees?

You usually pay a one-off setup plus any plate or tooling your structure needs, then a per-unit price that falls as quantity rises. The 3D mockup is free; material samples carry a small fee that is credited against bulk. The quote lists each line so the total is clear before you commit.

QWhat certifications and material compliance do you offer?

Our factory holds ISO 9001, and FSC-certified board and food-contact grades are sourced on request. Compliance paperwork for retail or cross-border selling is available on request. Tell us the market and we’ll confirm exactly which certification is backed with documents.

QWhat won’t this factory be a good fit for?

Packaging is the focus, not private-label products, so it’s not a fit for OEM goods, fashion bags or apparel. Unlimited free revisions with no brief aren’t offered, and a finished sample can’t ship overnight. If it isn’t a fit, we’ll say so and save you the cost.

QIs my design and tooling kept confidential?

Yes. We work under NDA on request. The dieline, artwork and any tooling are assigned to your product and never shown to or reused for another brand. When you reorder, the master file stays with you in case you ever move suppliers.
