D3 · §6Four thicknesses, drawn to scale — a bar chart instead of four platesThe four materials differ by two orders of magnitude in what they add: a board is millimetres, a foil mark is microns. Drawn against a common baseline at true scale, that difference is the argument — it is the board doing the work, and the finish doing the selling. Cost: it replaces the four material photographs with a diagram, and it is the one device here with no product shot in it.
