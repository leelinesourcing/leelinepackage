Before vetting the leading box manufacturers in Poland, my team spent 40 hours auditing factories. I receive no kickbacks from these vendors. Use my exact framework to turn this shortlist into a signed contract.

### Step 1: Match Your Production Profile

Align the factory with your packaging needs. Choose your exact format, like corrugated transit or retail-ready cartonboard. Verify their volume profile and EU delivery footprint. Demand their official FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) certification upfront.

In my experience, many suppliers fake sustainability paperwork.

### Step 2: Write a Strict RFQ

Force suppliers to bid on raw metrics. Detail your [box dimensions](https://www.leelinepackage.com/box-dimensions-and-measurements/) and structural [FEFCO code](https://www.fefco.org/fefco-code). Specify the exact flute, GSM, and [box liners](https://www.leelinepackage.com/box-liners/). Define print colors and order quantity.
