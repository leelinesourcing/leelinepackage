## What Are Tyvek Bags?

<iframe class="post-embed" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/tutez8HbHNM" width="100%" height="480" frameborder="0" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share"></iframe>

Tyvek bags are reusable bags made from DuPont™ Tyvek®. This is a special type of polyethylene fiber. You get strong bags that feel super light. This material works great for industries that need protective barriers. It blocks particles, moisture, punctures, and chemicals.

Plus, it breathes and produces very little lint.

**Key Tyvek Bag Grades You Can Choose**

- **1422A**: Need sterile protection? This grade handles heat sterilization. It releases few particles. We make it in ISO 7 cleanrooms.
- **1421B**: Best for flexible uses. You don't need any special treatment. Perfect for PureClean™ sterilization bags.
- **1073B**: Want top safety and cleanliness? Pharma and food companies use this grade often. It meets strict GMP and ISO rules.

## Material Properties of Tyvek Relevant to Bags

Wondering what makes Tyvek bags different? The material sets them apart for demanding B2B uses. These bags use 100% high-density polyethylene (HDPE) fibers. The fibers are flash-spun and bonded. This creates up to 10x the tensile strength of paper. Your bags resist tearing and punctures.

They hold up even when wet or roughly handled. Cotton and polypropylene don't match Tyvek's long-term toughness. Plus, Tyvek stays flexible down to –73°C.

**Lightweight, Flexible & Eco-Friendly**

Tyvek bags weigh up to 43% less than cotton bags. You cut both shipping and material costs. Weights start as low as 1.25 oz/yd². Pack them, fold them, store them—all easy. The surfaces are crisp and great for printing your branding. These bags are 100% recyclable as HDPE #2.

No plasticizers. No hazardous chemicals. This supports your eco-commitments.

**Water, Chemical & Print Resistant**

Tyvek bags are waterproof but also breathable. They block moisture, mold, and microbes. Air or steam? That passes through. This works well in many environments. The material resists chemicals strongly. UV won't degrade it. Processing for bulk bag manufacturing is simple.

## People Also Ask About What Are Tyvek Bags?

**1. What is Tyvek made of?**

Tyvek is made from flash‑spun continuous fibers of high‑density polyethylene that are laid in random patterns and heat‑bonded into a strong sheet. Tyvek is made from just one polymer (HDPE) and has no plasticizers.

**2. Why are Tyvek bags so durable?**

Tyvek’s tightly woven HDPE fibers provide high strength. This makes it tough against tears, punctures, and wear, much more than regular paper. This structure lets Tyvek bags withstand repeated folding, crumpling, and daily use without easily ripping.​

**3. Are Tyvek bags waterproof?**

Tyvek is very water-resistant. Its dense fibers block liquid water and many contaminants, keeping contents dry. Its microporous structure lets water vapor and air pass through. This makes the material breathable, unlike plastic film, which is fully waterproof.
