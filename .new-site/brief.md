# LeelinePackage — Custom Packaging Site Redesign Brief

## The Business

LeelinePackage (Wuhan, China) is a specialized **custom-packaging manufacturer / aggregator** — the "Structural Brand Architect." They turn plain shipping containers into viral unboxing assets: engineering the box, not just printing on it.

- **Products:** corrugated mailer boxes, rigid gift boxes, folding cartons, flexible pouches/mylar bags, eco-friendly/recycled packaging, display boxes, paper bags, tin/acrylic boxes, satin bags, labels & stickers.
- **Services:** Structural engineering (custom dielines & inserts), print finishes (foil, spot UV, emboss/deboss), custom inserts (foam/paper/molded pulp), flat-pack freight optimization.
- **Differentiator — "Protect & Impress":** structural integrity (no "crushed box" disasters) + premium finishes at a fraction of Western cost + factory-direct lower MOQ (from 500 units).

## The Brand Archetype

**The Guardian Architect.** Both protector of the product and builder of the brand image. Tone is **Structural, Premium, Assuring** — technical yet creative, focused on material specs, print finishes, structural integrity, load-bearing, and the unboxing experience.
