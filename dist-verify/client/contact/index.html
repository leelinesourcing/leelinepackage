Founder’s note

Hi, I’m Ray, the Founder of LeelinePackage

Having spent over a decade in global trade, I saw firsthand the challenges businesses face in finding sustainable, reliable packaging solutions. The complexity of sourcing eco-friendly materials and ensuring quality was a constant hurdle. I knew there had to be a better way.

So, I created LeelinePackage in 2015. It’s a one-stop solution for brands wanting to bring their packaging ideas to life. We manage everything from design and sourcing to production and logistics. This lets our clients grow their business without the hassle of dealing with many suppliers.

LeelinePackage is more than packaging; it’s a partnership. We’re committed to delivering high-quality, eco-conscious solutions that meet each client’s unique needs. Our mission is to empower businesses with packaging that’s functional, sustainable, and beautifully crafted, supporting their success every step of the way.
