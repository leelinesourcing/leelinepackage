10 Different Types Of Box Displays To Elevate Your Retail Merchandising

By Lofty ShenSenior Packaging Engineer·January 9, 2026·11 min read

You walk into a retail store. Within seconds, a bright display catches your eye. It pulls you toward products you didn’t even know you needed. That’s strategic merchandising at work.

In today’s retail world, the different types of box displays you choose can make or break your sales. Research shows that smart point-of-purchase displays do more than grab attention. They trigger impulse buys and can boost sales by up to 140%.

Are you a wholesaler helping retailers maximize shelf impact? Or a store owner working to boost conversions? Either way, knowing your display options matters.

This guide covers 10 different types of box displays solutions. We focus on customizable, budget-friendly, and earth-friendly cardboard and corrugated options. They deliver results without draining your wallet. The right display box can transform your retail strategy—so let’s explore how.

1. Countertop Display Boxes

Countertop display boxes work great for merchandising. They’re one of the most popular Different Types Of Box Displays. Place them near checkouts to catch shopper eyes and boost impulse buys.

You can pick from standard sizes: 7”×8”×1.25”, 10”×10”×1.5”, or 12”×10”×2”. Got lightweight items like cosmetics or snacks? Standard paperboard works well. E-flute corrugated is another good choice. Both give you solid strength at a fair cost. Heavier products need more support.

Corrugated board counter boxes hold up better. They also make in-store restocking (PDQ) faster.

Print options? You get full-color CMYK, spot PMS, or plain finishes. Pick gloss, matte, UV, or embossed lamination to make your brand pop. Lead times run 4-8 business days. Need it faster? Rush service is available. No minimum orders required. Store staff can set them up quick.

This makes them flexible for most retailers.

Custom Countertop Display Boxes

2. Floor-Standing Display Boxes

Floor-standing display boxes are one of the key types of box displays, ideal for showcasing products and graphics in high-traffic areas. These units are sturdy, large, and great for B2B use.

Take a poster display box with a steel frame—it stands 79” high and shows up to 48 graphics across 24 swing panels. Your visuals appear on both sides. The build supports top-loading for fast poster changes. Clear overlays protect your prints.

Modular Frame Options

Pick upright heights from 48” to 84”. Mix panel widths from 14” to 48” for the right brand fit. The modular MR series supports 1- to 4-sided channels. Sturdy steel bases add extra stability.

Flexible design and storage make floor-standing displays perfect for stores that change promotions often.

Expert Insight: I recommend selecting the type based on your product size, target location in the store, and goals—such as encouraging quick purchases with counters or maximizing visibility with floors—to effectively boost sales and customer engagement.

3. Pallet Display Boxes (Full, Half, or Quarter Pallet)

Pallet display boxes are a go-to choice for high-impact retail merchandising. You can pick full, half, or quarter pallet sizes to match your floor space and stock needs. In North America, full pallets measure 48” x 40”. Half pallets come in 24” x 40” or 48” x 20”.

Quarter pallets are 20” x 24” or 24” x 20”. For European markets, full pallets convert to 80 x 120 cm and quarter pallets to 60 x 40 cm. This flexibility works for local standards.

Retail-Ready Design & Compliance

Major retailers set strict pallet display rules. Walmart blocks quarter pallets. They limit half and full units to 43” total height (not counting header and pallet base). This keeps store aisles clear. Meijer wants half and quarter pallets shipped on top of full pallets.

You must wrap them securely with corrugated material for shipping. Meet these specs and retailers accept your products quickly. Your display goes up fast. Shoppers notice it right away.

Custom Pallet Display Boxes

4. Dump Bin Display Boxes

Dump bin display boxes work well for many retail setups. Pick from floor-standing, pallet-based, or countertop bins. Floor dump bins, like the 36” W x 32” H wire model, hold heavy bulk items. Cardboard types with headers (23.8” W x 42.1” H) support up to 22 lbs.

These suit lightweight products that sell fast.

Need more space? Basket-style stands come with four 18” diameter baskets. They hold up to 50 lbs total and spin for quick access. Pallet-based dump bins match Euro or American pallet standards (quarter, half, full). This makes storage and transport simple.

Cardboard bins ship flat (KDF) or already built. Both options set up fast.

Customize your branding, color, and size. Add removable headers for signs. Plastic or metal bins work best for heavy items. Corrugated bins cost less and fit short-term promotions. Your team can set these up quickly. Moving them around is easy too.

This gives you more ways to display your products.

Custom Dump Bin Display Boxes

5. Power Wing/Sidekick Display Boxes

Power wing/sidekick display boxes work great for getting your products noticed in busy store areas. These boxes hang on wire frames at end-caps or aisle sides. They put your products right at eye level.

Standard box sizes range from 14” x 5” x 24” (supports up to 25 lb) up to 14” x 5” x 48” (supports up to 50 lb), adapting to small or heavier items. Peg hook options let you display hanging merchandise in an organized way.

**Key Benefits **

  • Built for Strength and Display: Corrugated sidekicks come in single, double, or triple-wall construction (200–1500 microns thickness). Edge crush test ratings match your product weight. You get reliable support. Plus, they’re simple to set up in-store.
  • Better Branding Options: Print full-color graphics on laminated finishes for a professional look. Headers stay attached and make your display stand out on shelves.
  • Flexible, Cost-Effective Orders: MOQ as low as 100 pcs; trial orders accepted. Sidekicks ship flat for low freight costs. Setup takes just minutes.
  • Real Sales Impact: Retailers report higher sales from sidekick and power wing placement compared to standard shelving. The difference is clear.

Custom Sidekick Display Boxes

6. End Cap Display Boxes

End cap display boxes are among the most effective different types of box displays for retailers looking to maximize visibility. They put your products at the end of aisles. Shopper traffic there is about 4 times higher than in regular aisles.

This setup drives impulse buys and lifts sales.

Key Benefits

  • Superior Load-Bearing: Metal gondola end caps support up to 1,250 lb. They’re perfect for heavy items or bulk displays. Cardboard and corrugated end caps ship flat (knockdown or KDF). This cuts freight and storage costs.
  • Flexible Sizing: You get widths from 36 to 48 inches. Heights range from 54 to 84 inches. Standard base depths go from 16 to 24 inches. Need more stock? Deeper options are available.
  • Branding Opportunities: Add tiered “waterfall” shelves or headers. These show off your logo and increase shelf presence.
  • Quick Setup: Most end cap kits are freestanding and tool-free. They ship assembled in 3 to 5 business days.
  • Versatility: Pick metal, wood, or recycled plastic. All options work for indoor or outdoor use and meet environmental standards.

Custom End Cap Display Boxes

7. PDQ (Pretty Darn Quick) Tray Displays

PDQ tray displays give you a fast, ready-to-sell solution among the different types of box displays​. They meet big-box retailer standards, including Walmart. Each shelf of PDQ trays should be 0.5″ narrower than the total shelf width.

For a 48″ shelf, two trays at 23.75″ each (47.5″ total) fit right and prevent snagging side brackets.

Keep trays under 14″ depth or 50 lb per tray. This keeps them safe on the shelf. Pick corrugated board thickness based on your load. Single-wall (200–500 μm) works for light items. Double-wall (600–1000 μm) handles heavier products. Triple-wall (up to 1500 μm) gives you max stability.

Use Edge Crush Test ratings (32–44 ECT) and burst strength (200–400) based on your product’s weight. Each tray needs clear barcodes, shipping labels, and secured product placement. These displays boost efficiency. They make in-store setup simple.

Plus, they help you meet strict retailer requirements.

Custom PDQ (Pretty Darn Quick) Tray Displays

8. Hanging or Peg Hook Display Boxes

Hanging or peg hook display boxes give you a versatile way to showcase small, lightweight products. These boxes work well for many different types of box displays. Individual boxes come in custom sizes, from 2” to 4” in length. Floor stands offer multiple hooks and complete customization.

Choose from premium cardboard or corrugated options. Materials like oyster white board, kemi board, or recycled corrugated give you strength and sustainability. They also keep your brand consistent.

**Key Features **

  • Custom Fit: Set dimensions, hook hole shape, and spacing to match your product and pegboard layout. Pick from hang tabs, circular holes, or euro slots.
  • Sturdy Design: Reinforced hanging areas stop tearing. This works even with multiple facings or heavier sachets.
  • Flexible Hook Systems: Hooks come in glass-filled nylon, metal, or strong plastic. They work with pegboard and slatwall displays. Both 0.125” and 0.25” pegboards fit.
  • Branding Power: Print on all box surfaces using offset, digital, or UV methods. Finishes include soft-touch, anti-scratch lamination, foil stamping, and spot gloss. Your graphics will pop.
  • Compliance: Materials meet CE, BSCI, and ISO 9001 standards.

Hanging displays work great for accessories, sachets, snacks, or boxed items. You can show more SKUs in tight spaces. Plus, restocking becomes faster and easier.

Custom Peg Hook Display Boxes

9. Gravity Feed Display Boxes

Gravity feed display boxes represent one of the most different types of box displays for keeping products neatly organized and always front-facing on shelves. Pick from small sizes (5″ x 3.5″ x 6″, holds ~15 candy bars) to large ones (10.25″ x 5.25″ x 11″, holds ~35 snack pouches).

Match the size to your stock. These boxes work great in grocery stores, convenience shops, drug stores, electronics outlets, and beauty retailers.

Key Features

  • Auto-Restocking: Products slide forward as customers grab them. This boosts visibility. Plus, it cuts down restocking time.
  • Custom Sizing: Pick dimensions that fit your products. Use gravity bins for tea bags, energy shots, lipsticks, or tech gear.
  • Material Options: You get cardboard, plywood (like 3 mm Baltic birch, 5-slot units holding 20 boxes), clear polypropylene with magnetic doors, or acrylic bins.
  • Easy Placement: Go with countertop or floor models. Both fill and access easily.

Gravity feed boxes help you increase sales. They keep displays tidy and meet major retailers’ standards.

Themed and interactive custom display boxes are growing fast among different types of box displays​. They capture shopper attention during seasonal promotions and limited-edition launches. The global custom display packaging market will reach USD 24.7 billion in 2025.

Floor stand and pop-up/seasonal display boxes dominate high-traffic retail areas.

Most boxes use sustainable paper and paperboard. These materials hold over 62.5% market share. B2B users meet consumer and regulatory demands for green displays. Creative designs drive more engagement.

FMCG and consumer goods campaigns get the most benefits from these displays. They impact 4–6% of all consumer packaging. Global CAGR sits at 5.6% (2025–2035). China and India show top growth. Themed displays help you scale up for diverse regional campaigns.

They boost product visibility at retail right away.

People Also Ask About Different Types Of Box Displays

1. What materials are commonly used for box displays?

Most retail box displays use corrugated cardboard. This material is durable, lightweight, and easy to print on. Other options include acrylic (for premium, transparent cases), kraft paper (eco-friendly), or rigid board for luxury items.

2. Why use cardboard for box displays?

Cardboard is cost-effective, recyclable, customizable with printing, and easy to assemble/disassemble. It’s strong enough for temporary promotions yet lightweight for shipping and setup in stores.

3. How do box displays help in retail marketing?

Box displays increase product visibility, encourage impulse purchases (up to 60% of buys in some categories), reinforce branding, and highlight promotions. Strategic placement in high-traffic areas maximizes sales impact.
