By Lofty ShenSenior Packaging Engineer·October 30, 2025·12 min read

Did you know the global demand for Flexible Intermediate Bulk Containers (FIBCs), also known as jumbo bags, is set to hit $10 billion by 2027? This growth is fueled by strong sectors like agriculture, chemicals, and construction.

These versatile giants are revolutionizing bulk material handling worldwide.

Jumbo bags are large, durable sacks designed to transport and store up to 2,000 kg of dry goods safely and efficiently. Their importance lies in reducing packaging costs, minimizing waste, and enhancing logistics for industries reliant on bulk shipments.

Gujarat is India’s top spot for making premium jumbo bags. It has modern facilities and high-quality standards. In this guide, we’ll explore the top 8 Junbo bag manufacturers for diverse global sourcing.
